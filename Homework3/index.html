Welcome to the Pennsylvania Botanical Garden

About Us

Founded in 1858, the Pennsylvania Botanical Garden is the largest and most well-known botanical garden in central Pennsylvania. Known for housing over 200 species of native plants, our 250-acre walk-through garden is a premire destination for plant enthusiasts, families, and tourists of all ages.

Our Mission

We strive to educate the public about the importance of native plants and their role in protecting wildlife and maintaining healthy ecosystems. Through guided tours, online classes, and preservation of Pennsylvania's native plant population, we hope to inspire you to learn more about how to care for and nurture the environment.
