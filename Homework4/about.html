About

This is a simple web application that provides information about native plants in Pennsylvania. The app includes a plant finder feature that allows users to search for native plants by name, as well as a details page that provides more information about each plant.

Who we are

We are a group of students and researchers who are passionate about native plants and their importance in the ecosystem. Our goal is to educate the public about native plants and encourage their use in landscaping and gardening.
