Native plants are plants that naturally occur in a particular region. They provide food and shelter for local wildlife.

Butterfly Weed

Scientific Name: Asclepias tuberosa
Commonly known as butterfly weed, this plant is a favorite of monarch butterflies. It thrives in sunny areas and well-drained soil.

Height 2-3 feet
Bloom Time Summer
Soil Type Well-drained
Sunlight Full Sun
